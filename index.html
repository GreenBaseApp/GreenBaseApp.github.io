Lawn care software, built by someone who mows lawns.

GreenBase keeps your whole lawn care business in your pocket — customers, schedule, routes, invoices, and expenses — without the bloat and the price tag of software built for big companies.

What it does

Customers — every customer with their rate, how often they're serviced, gate codes, and service history. See at a glance who's due and who's overdue.
Routes — group customers by day or area, plan the driving order, and check off stops as you go. Finishing a stop logs what you earned.
Invoices — build a clean invoice in seconds, send it by text or email, and send a one-tap reminder when it hasn't been paid.
Money — real earnings from completed jobs, expenses by category, and what you actually made this month.
Your crew — text a crew member an invite code and they see the same customer list on their phone, updated live.

Who it's for

Solo operators and small crews who want to stop running a business out of a notebook and a group text.
