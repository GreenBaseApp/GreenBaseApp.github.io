Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

GreenBase LLC ("we", "us") makes the GreenBase app, a tool for lawn care businesses to manage customers, jobs, invoices, and expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information you provide

Account information. An email address and password, used only to create and secure your account.

Business records you enter. Customer names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, service notes, invoices, job history, and expenses. This data belongs to you. We store it so it can sync between your devices and with team members you invite.

Receipt photos are stored only on the device that took them and are not uploaded to our servers.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored on your device and synced to our cloud database (hosted by Supabase) over encrypted connections. Access is restricted so that only your account — and team members you explicitly invite — can see your business's data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or your customers' data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, except the service providers that host our infrastructure (Supabase) and process payments (Apple, Google).

Payments

Subscriptions, where offered, are processed by the app store on your device. We never see your payment card details.

Data deletion

You can delete customers, invoices, jobs, and expenses in the app at any time; deletions also remove the cloud copies. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email greenbasesupport@gmail.com and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Children

GreenBase is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
