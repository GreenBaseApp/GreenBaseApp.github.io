Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

GreenBase LLC ("we", "us") makes the GreenBase app, a tool for lawn care businesses to manage customers, jobs, invoices, and expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information you provide

Account information. An email address and password. On iPhone you can instead choose Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google, in which case Apple or Google share your email address and an account identifier with us; the Android app offers email and password only. Used only to create and secure your account. An account is optional: without one, everything you enter stays on your device.

Business records you enter. Customer names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, service notes, invoices, job history, and expenses. This data belongs to you. We store it so it can sync between your devices and with team members you invite.

Receipt photos are stored only on the device that took them and are not uploaded to our servers.

Location. The Android app does not use your phone's location and does not ask for permission to. When you plan a route it hands your customers' addresses to Google Maps, and Google Maps works the route out from wherever you are — your location is handled by Google Maps under its own policy and never reaches us. On iPhone the app does use your location, so the route can start where you are; it is used on your device, sent to Apple Maps to work out driving times, and we do not store it. On both, customer addresses are turned into map coordinates by the phone's maps service, and those coordinates are saved with the customer so routes can be planned.

Messages you send us. If you write to us from inside the app, we keep your message and your account email so we can reply.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Their content does not pass through our servers.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored on your device and synced to our cloud database (hosted by Supabase) over encrypted connections. Access is restricted so that only your account — and team members you explicitly invite — can see your business's data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or your customers' data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you, and we do not share your data with third parties, except the service providers that run parts of the app: Supabase (our database), Stripe (card payments, only if your business turns them on), Apple (subscriptions, maps and Sign in with Apple on iPhone) and Google (subscriptions and maps on Android, and Continue with Google where it is offered).

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by the app store on your device — the App Store on iPhone, Google Play on Android. We never see your payment card details.

Card payments for your customers. If your business turns on card payments, you connect your own Stripe account. You give your identity and bank details to Stripe directly, on Stripe's own pages; we never see them. We store your Stripe account ID and whether card payments are switched on. When you send a pay-by-card invoice, we send Stripe the invoice number and amount so it can create the payment page, and Stripe tells us when it has been paid. Your customer's card details go to Stripe, never to us. Stripe's handling of that information is covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

Data deletion

You can delete customers, invoices, jobs, and expenses in the app at any time; deletions also remove the cloud copies. To delete your entire account and everything on it, open the menu on the Customers screen and tap Delete Account — this takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. If you can't reach the app, email greenbasesupport@gmail.com and we will complete the deletion within 30 days. See Delete your account for the full details.

Children

GreenBase is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
