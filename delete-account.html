Delete your GreenBase account

You can delete your account and everything on it at any time. There are two ways.

If you've been using GreenBase without an account, there's nothing here for you to do — your work is stored only on your own phone and we never received it. Deleting the app removes it.

In the app

This is the fastest way, and it happens immediately.

  1. Open GreenBase and make sure you're logged in.
  2. On the Customers screen, tap Menu in the top left.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you're returned to the login screen.

By email

If you can't get into the app — a lost phone, or a password you can't reset — email greenbasesupport@gmail.com from the address your account uses and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days at the latest and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your login and email address
  • Every customer on your account, including their names, addresses and phone numbers
  • Every invoice, job record and expense
  • Your route groups and business settings
  • Any crew invitations you created

None of it is recoverable. We don't keep a copy, and we can't restore an account once it's gone, so export or write down anything you still need first.

If you're on a crew

If you were invited to someone else's business, deleting your account removes your login and your access to that business. It does not delete their customers or their records — those belong to the account that owns the business.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription, because subscriptions are billed by the app store rather than by us. Cancel it in your Google Play or Apple account's subscription settings before deleting, or you may keep being charged for an account that no longer exists.

Receipt photos

Receipt photos are stored on your own phone and never uploaded to us, so they aren't part of your account. Deleting the app removes them.

Questions about any of this: greenbasesupport@gmail.com.
