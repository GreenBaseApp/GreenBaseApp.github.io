Common questions

How does syncing work?

Your customers, invoices, jobs, and expenses save to your phone instantly and sync to the cloud whenever you open the customer list or pull down to refresh it.

How do I add a crew member?

Open the menu, tap Team, then "Text an Invite." Text them the code — they enter it on the same Team screen on their phone and your customer list appears for them.

How do I plan a route?

Tap Route, pick a route group (like a day of the week), then Plan Route. Work down the numbered list and tap each stop when it's done.

Is my data private?

Yes. Only your account — and the crew you invite — can see your business. We don't sell data or run ads. Receipt photos never leave your phone. Full details on our Privacy page.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" We'll email you a 6-digit code to reset it.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the app store on your device, in your account's subscription settings.

How do I delete my account?

Email greenbasesupport@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
